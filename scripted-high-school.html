What got cut from the tape

Talking first. Asking. Nerves. Laughing. Condoms and birth control. Stopping when someone’s not into it. Only 1 in 3 teens who had seen porn had ever seen anyone ask. Choking shows up so often it has spread into real life. Pressure on the neck can cut off blood to the brain within seconds, and there’s no safe way to do it.

If nobody hands you a script, porn will

Researchers call them sexual scripts: the playbook in your head for what happens and who does what. More teens said they learned a lot from a parent or trusted adult (47%) than from porn (27%).

Skip these
  • “Real guys always know what to do”Almost everyone is nervous at first. Asking is confidence.
  • “It’s just a video”It can still become the script. Check it against real life.
  • Shame as a planShame makes a habit harder to change. Setup changes work better.
