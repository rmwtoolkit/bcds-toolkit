Part 1 · Understand it

What vaping does to a growing brain

Sound familiar?Real facts, not a lecture.
  • You've seen kids vaping in the bathroom, on the bus, or at a party.
  • Someone offered you a hit and said it's "basically just flavored water."
  • You've seen videos online of people doing vape tricks.
  • You're curious about it, or you already tried it.
  • Someone in your family vapes or smokes.
  • You're worried about a friend who vapes a lot.

Whatever your experience, this is information, not a lecture. You get to make your own choices, and you deserve to make them with real facts.

What's actually in a vape

Tap each one.

🧪Nicotine

Most vapes have nicotine, often a lot. Some single disposables have about as much as a pack of cigarettes or more. Some labeled "nicotine free" have been found to contain it.

🌿Sometimes THC

Some vapes contain THC, the part of cannabis that gets you high. They're often much stronger than people expect.

☁️Other chemicals

The "vapor" is an aerosol with tiny particles and flavor chemicals that go deep into your lungs. It's not water vapor.

Why it matters for your brainYour brain is still under construction.
Your brain is still building itself.
Brains keep developing until about age 25. The parts for focus, decision-making, and impulse control are some of the last to finish.
Nicotine changes that wiring.
Research shows nicotine can affect attention, learning, and memory in developing brains, and makes the brain more sensitive to other addictions.
Addiction happens faster for teens.
Teen brains get hooked on nicotine more quickly than adult brains. Many teens who vape say they want to quit but find it hard.
It can make stress and anxiety worse.
Lots of people vape to calm down. Nicotine withdrawal causes anxiety and irritability, so the vape feels like relief, but it's partly relieving the stress it created.
📢 The marketing trickCandy flavors aren't an accident.

Bright colors, candy flavors, sleek designs that look like USB drives or highlighters, influencers posting clouds. None of that is an accident. It's designed to make vaping look harmless and fun, especially to young people. Tobacco companies have also aimed ads at Black, Latino, and LGBTQ+ communities for decades.

The worst part: the companies that make vapes earn more when you need them. Getting hooked young is exactly what keeps them in business.

Part 2 · Practice the skills

Finding your way through it

What actually helps

Know the facts. Have your line ready.

Tap one to see how.

🧠 Know the facts.

Knowing what's in a vape makes it easier to decide for yourself, without guessing or going along.

💬 Have a line ready.

"Nah, I'm good." "I've got practice." "My parents would smell it." You don't owe anyone a long explanation.

🌬 Find other ways to handle stress.

Movement, music, breathing, prayer or meditation, talking to someone. Anything that calms you without a crash afterward.

👥 Hang with people who don't push.

Real friends accept no. If someone keeps pressuring you, Saying No Without Losing Your Friends can help.

🔍 Notice your triggers.

If you already vape, notice when: stress, boredom, being with certain people. Knowing triggers is step one to changing them.

🆓 Get free help to quit.

A parent or caregiver, counselor, nurse, or doctor can help you quit, no matter how long you've vaped.

Try itFlavored water? Let's check.

Tap an answer for each one.

"Vapes are just flavored water vapor."

"Teens get addicted to nicotine faster than adults."

"Vaping helps with anxiety."

"If a vape says nicotine free, it definitely is."

Try itTwo real moments. What's your move?

Pick a move that works for you.

An older kid in the bathroom holds out a vape and says, "Try it, it tastes like mango."

There's usually more than one good answer. The best one is the one you'll actually do.

What doesn't actually helpThese usually keep the habit going.
😨 Scare tactics.

Fear doesn't usually change behavior. Facts and support do.

🙈 "I can stop anytime."

Most people who get hooked thought that too.

🔁 Switching to "lighter" vapes.

Lower nicotine can still keep the habit going.

🤐 Hiding it and struggling alone.

Counselors help students quit all the time. It's about your health, not getting in trouble.

Get help right away if

  • You or a friend has trouble breathing, chest pain, or a cough that won't go away after vaping.
  • Someone gets very sick, confused, or passes out after using a vape, especially one that might contain THC.
  • You feel like you can't get through the day without vaping.

For an emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room. Poison Control is 1-800-222-1222: free, private, with interpreters in many languages. See Get help below.

Other ways in

People have been selling things to kids for a long time. These stories saw it coming.

📖 Read

The Lorax · Dr. Seuss, 1971 Throwback

The Once-ler sells Thneeds, "a Fine-Something-That-All-People-Need," and gets rich while everything around him falls apart. Read it again with candy-flavored vapes in mind.

🎬 Watch

Pinocchio · Disney, 1940 Throwback

The Coachman lures boys to Pleasure Island with free cigars and fun, and he gets paid once they're stuck there. That's the marketing trick from the lesson. Rated G.

WALL-E · Pixar, 2008

One giant company sells people everything, and its ads keep them sitting, sipping, and buying. Watch how bright and friendly the ads look. Rated G.

🎧 Listen

What Do You Do with the Mad That You Feel? · Fred Rogers, 1968 Throwback

Mister Rogers asked kids what to do with big feelings long before anyone vaped to calm down. His answers didn't need a charger.

🖼️ Look

Guts · Raina Telgemeier, 2019

Raina's worry shows up as stomachaches. A therapist teaches her breathing and grounding, and later she teaches them to her class. Other ways to handle stress, in a graphic memoir.

✏️ Make your own

Design a fake vape ad the way the companies would: bright colors, a candy flavor, a cool name. Then flip the page and make the honest version. What is it really selling?
