Is This a Good Friend?

Some friends charge you up. Some drain you. Most do a little of both.

🔒 Nothing you tap here is saved or sent.

Myth or fact?

“A real friend would never hurt me.”

AnswerMyth. Good friends hurt each other sometimes. The question: do they own it and change?

Good-news practice

Good news is where friends show up.

When it lands: glad for you, asks a question, brings it up later. When it misses: a shrug, holes poked, their story, a new subject.

Dev

Wants your location on all the time “so I know you’re safe.” Sulks for a day whenever you turn it off.

Caring doesn’t need a live map. Try: “I’m keeping location off. I’ll text when I’m home.”

Nia

Pulls you aside and says your group-chat joke about a classmate’s accent came across as mean.

She told you privately and gave you a chance to fix it. The friend who says the hard thing kindly is a keeper.

Luis

A junior on your team drives you home from practice and takes the back roads way too fast. Everyone cheers. So do you.

Good guy, risky moment. Say it once (“can you slow down? I get carsick”) and line up another ride.

More, if you want it

Good friends don’t all look alike

Teasing, quiet, loud, formal. Families and cultures set different rules for friendship. Who you are shapes what “close” looks like.

Gray areas

Talking it to death, the rollercoaster, the put-down, the controller. Any friend can have a bad week. The pattern over months is what tells you something.

Other ways in

Stories have been testing friendships for a long time. A couple of these go back to when you were small.

  • ReadThe Hate U Give Angie Thomas, 2017Starr's friend Hailey keeps making comments about race and calls them jokes. When Starr says it hurts and it keeps happening, she has to decide what this friendship is costing her.Heads up: The story starts with a police shooting of Starr's childhood friend. It includes violence, death and strong language.
  • WatchToy Story Pixar, 1995 ThrowbackBuzz shows up and everyone loves him. Woody can't be glad for him. It's a good-news moment gone wrong in a big way, and then fixed. Rated G.
  • WatchParks and Recreation NBC, 2009-2015Leslie Knope is the top-left box on the good-news grid. When Ann has good news, Leslie gets more excited than Ann does.Heads up: Rated TV-PG. Some sexual humor; Common Sense Media says 13 and up.
  • ListenStand by Me Ben E. King, 1961"I won't be afraid, just as long as you stand by me." Researchers found the same thing with a best friend in the room: stress hits softer.
  • LookPeanuts: Lucy and the football Charles M. Schulz, 1952 on ThrowbackEvery time, Lucy promises she'll hold the ball, and every time, she pulls it away. Charlie Brown keeps coming back. That's the rollercoaster, in four panels.
  • MakeDraw the four-box good-news grid. This week, when a friend shares good news, put a dot in the box you landed in. Do the same for how they answered yours.
