More, if you want it

Two different situations

One is pressure from someone you know: “if you trusted me, you would.” The other is a scam run by strangers, built to get one image and then money. Both are someone else doing something wrong. Both have a way out.

How the scam works

The account looks like someone your age, and the photo they send first is usually stolen. Real people rarely go from hello to nudes in an hour. Once they have an image, the tone flips: screenshots of your followers and a demand for money. Paying almost never ends it.

What the law says

Sexual images of anyone under 18 are illegal to create, ask for, keep, or share, even when everyone agreed. The federal TAKE IT DOWN Act requires covered platforms to remove reported images within 48 hours. If someone is threatening you, the law treats you as the victim.

Skip these
  • Paying to make it stopIt tells them you’ll pay again.
  • “I’ll delete it after”Nobody can promise that about a copy.
  • Keeping it secretShame is what the threat runs on. Telling one adult takes it away.
