More, if you want it

How common it is

About 1 in 12 high schoolers who dated experienced physical dating violence in the past year. Among teens with dating experience, 31% said a partner checked up on them several times a day and 13% had a partner demand their passwords. Digital abuse is abuse.

Healthy, unhealthy, abusive

Healthy: you both keep your own friends, plans and privacy, and you can disagree without fear. Unhealthy: jealousy runs the mood and pressure replaces asking. Abusive: control, fear or harm. These patterns show up in friendships and families too.

If you decide to leave

Tell someone first. End it in public or by phone. Change passwords and turn off location sharing. If a partner threatens to hurt themselves, you still don’t have to stay. Tell a trusted adult, and they can call or text 988.

Skip these
  • “Jealousy means they care”A flash is human. Using it to control you isn’t love.
  • “It’s only abuse if it’s physical”Monitoring, threats and isolation do real harm.
  • “If I’m a better partner, it’ll stop”Control isn’t caused by your mistakes.
Other ways in

Writers have been drawing the line between love and control for a long time. Two of these you probably met as a kid.

  • ReadThe Giving Tree Shel Silverstein, 1964 ThrowbackThe boy takes and takes, down to the trunk, and the tree keeps saying she's happy. Read it again with the lesson's question: is her world getting bigger or smaller? People have argued about this book for sixty years.
  • ReadCoraline Neil Gaiman, 2002 ThrowbackThe Other Mother has better food and all the attention Coraline wants. Then she wants to sew buttons over Coraline's eyes so she'll stay forever. Step one of the control ladder, then step five.Heads up: Scary in places. The 2009 movie is rated PG for thematic elements, scary images, some language and suggestive humor.
  • WatchPride & Prejudice Joe Wright, 2005Wickham is charming and tells a great story about himself. Darcy is awkward and quietly does the right thing. What people do tells you more than how they come across. Rated PG.
  • ListenSince U Been Gone Kelly Clarkson, 2004The relief after a bad relationship ends: she can breathe for the first time. Play it after you answer the three questions honestly.
  • MakeDraw the control ladder from Part 1 using a story you know, from a movie or a book. Put one moment from it on each rung. Then draw the exit: where could someone have stepped off?
