Friendship Breakups

Nobody died. It still feels like grief.

Guess first

100 friendships start in 7th grade. How many last to senior year?

AnswerAbout 1. Fewer than 1 in 4 even made it to the next school year. A friendship can be real and still have an end date.

Why it hurts this much

Your brain files it like an injury.

No songs. No sympathy cards. Same hallway. The hurt is real, not dramatic.

Repair, reset or release?

Maya and Jordan

Best friends since 6th grade. Junior year, Jordan starts a demanding dual-enrollment program and cancels most plans. Maya feels dropped.

Worth a try. Tell Jordan she misses him and suggest one standing plan. Going quiet to see if he notices backfires.

Eli

A friend screenshotted a private message Eli sent about his family and shared it with the group. The friend apologized. Two weeks later, it happened again.

Step back, stay civil, stop sharing private things. Sharing the friend’s stuff back keeps it going.

Lena

Ended a friendship that had become draining. Now she feels guilty every time she sees her former friend eating alone.

A short, kind hello without going back. The guilt doesn’t prove she got it wrong.

More, if you want it

Mostly, people grow different

Different classes, teams, friends and interests end more friendships than fights do.

One ending can reach a lot of places

Who you are shapes how far it ripples: shared family friends, a team, a faith group, a group chat. It also shapes where you find your footing.

Other ways in

Songs and stories mostly cover the dating kind of breakup. These cover the other kind.

  • ReadFangirl Rainbow Rowell, 2013Cath and her twin Wren did everything together. At college Wren wants space, and Cath has to grieve a closeness that's not coming back the same way.Heads up: Includes college drinking, including a night that ends in the hospital, and a parent's mental health crisis.
  • WatchToy Story 3 Pixar, 2010 ThrowbackAndy is leaving for college and the toys have to face that he's outgrown them. The last scene is a goodbye done with kindness. Rated G.
  • WatchBend It Like Beckham Gurinder Chadha, 2002Jess and Jules are teammates and best friends until they fall for the same coach. It blows up, and then they have to decide what they want.Heads up: Rated PG-13 for language and sexual content.
  • ListenGood Riddance (Time of Your Life) Green Day, 1997Billie Joe Armstrong wrote it after a breakup. It's about letting something end and still being glad it happened.
  • ListenBad Blood Taylor Swift, 2014Swift has said it's about another musician, a former friend, and not a boyfriend. It's the angry stage of the blowup. Notice it doesn't stay there forever.
  • MakeMake a playlist in two halves. The first half is for letting it be sad. The second half is for the day you say yes to one new thing. Leave a gap between them for the waves.
