For the guys

The Movember Toolbox

You've got a toolbox for a flat tire or a slipped chain. This one's for the stuff guys don't say out loud.

Pick one tool, try it once this week, and see what happens. Use them in any order. Skip what doesn't fit.

Nothing you tap or type on this page is saved or sent.

Tool 1fits: any day

Find the word

"Fine" and "tired" cover a lot of ground. Tap the word that's closest to today.

Saying it to yourself counts.

Tool 2fits: a talk you've been putting off

Side by side

A lot of guys talk easier shoulder to shoulder than face to face. Pick a setting, and bring one true sentence with you.

One true sentence

  • "This week's been heavier than I've let on."
  • "Can I tell you something kind of random?"
  • "I've been in my head a lot lately."
Tool 3fits: a friend who seems off

Check on a friend

Send one

  • "You good? You seemed off today."
  • "Haven't seen you in a while. Food this weekend?"
  • "No pressure to talk, I'm around."

If he opens up, say

  • "Thanks for telling me."
  • "That sounds rough. How long has it been like this?"
  • "Want to talk to someone about it? I'll go with you."

If a friend talks about hurting himself, tell an adult today, even if he asks you to keep it quiet. Never keep a secret when someone isn't safe. Strong men speak up.

More, if you want it

Why guys go quiet

Dr. Niobe Way at NYU interviewed hundreds of boys. Younger boys talk about their best friends like family. By late high school, many have picked up the rules (be cool, need nobody, keep it light) and lost those friendships, and they miss them.

The numbers

In a 2025 CDC survey, about 1 in 4 high school boys had felt sad or hopeless for weeks at a time. About half of those boys said they didn't need help, and fewer than 1 in 4 had talked to a counselor.

Your family, your culture

Every family has rules about feelings. Some say "man up" or "be strong for your mom." Some say "we keep it in the house." Some talk about everything. Some of those rules protect you, and some might be worth updating. Which ones did you grow up with? Which would you keep?
