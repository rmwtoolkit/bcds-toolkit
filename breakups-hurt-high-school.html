What would help most?

Nate

Two weeks after the breakup, his ex texts at 11:40 p.m.: “I miss you. Can we talk?” He’s still hurting.

Replying at midnight is the pull talking. Wait until morning, then decide what he actually wants.

Ava

Her ex is now dating someone in their friend group. They all sit at the same lunch table.

Calling it out in the group chat makes it bigger. Sit with other friends for a while, and tell one close friend the truth.

Sam

He’s dated someone for five months and wants to end it. He doesn’t want to be cruel.

Fading out, or having a friend tell them, feels easier and lands worse. Say it clearly and kindly, in person or on a call if it’s safe.

More, if you want it

Why it pulls

In brain scans of people recently rejected but still in love, the reward system lit up at a photo of their ex. That’s why checking their story feels like a pull more than a decision. Short, secret and first relationships can hurt a lot.

You’ll probably feel better sooner than you think

College students predicted how upset they’d be after a breakup. Most overestimated it. If the low isn’t lifting after a few weeks, that’s a signal to get more help, not a weakness.

Skip these
  • “If it was short, it shouldn’t hurt this much”How much you invested matters more than how long it lasted.
  • “Someone new will fix it”A distraction helps for a while. It doesn’t rebuild you.
  • “I ended it, so I don’t get to be sad”You can lose something you chose to leave.
Other ways in

Every generation writes the breakup song again. Here are a few that get the hard part right.

  • ReadWhy We Broke Up Daniel Handler, art by Maira Kalman, 2011Min writes Ed a long letter and drops it off with a box of everything from their six weeks together. It's the message you're not sending, written all the way out. A Printz Honor book.Heads up: Frank about sex and teen relationships, though not graphic. Librarians often suggest it for older high schoolers.
  • Watch(500) Days of Summer Marc Webb, 2009Watch the split screen where Tom's expectations play next to what actually happens. He has to rethink the story before he can get back to architecture, the thing that was his before her.Heads up: Rated PG-13 for sexual material and language, with some drinking.
  • WatchLegally Blonde Robert Luketic, 2001Warner dumps Elle, so she gets into Harvard Law to win him back. By the end she wants something of her own, and him far less. That's rebuilding me.Heads up: Rated PG-13 for language and sexual references.
  • ListenWhen She Loved Me Sarah McLachlan, written by Randy Newman, 1999 ThrowbackJessie's song from Toy Story 2, about Emily slowly outgrowing her. Proof your reward system doesn't check the calendar, even for a cowgirl doll.
  • ListenDancing On My Own Robyn, 2010She's in the corner of the club watching her ex with someone new, and she can't stop looking. Every check resets the clock. This is what that feels like.
  • MakeMake the box from Why We Broke Up, on paper. List or sketch five things from the relationship. Next to each one, decide whether to keep it or let it go. Then add one thing that was yours before them.
