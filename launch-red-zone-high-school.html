More, if you want it

Why the first months

Researchers call the stretch from move-in to Thanksgiving the red zone. Crowds, heavy drinking and unfamiliar places are the conditions. With no friend group yet, nobody notices you didn’t come home. People who commit assault use that. It’s on them.

Mostly someone they know

About 9 in 10 college survivors knew the person: a classmate, a friend, a date, someone from the floor. It happens to every gender. Men are often the least likely to tell anyone.

Alcohol is a risk factor, never the cause

At least half of sexual assaults among college students involve alcohol. Being drunk never makes it the survivor’s fault, and someone who is incapacitated can’t consent.
