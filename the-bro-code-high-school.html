Other ways in

Questions about what makes a man strong are old. These have better answers than your feed.

  • ReadOf Boys and Men Richard V. Reeves, 2022Reeves takes guys' real struggles seriously, in school, work and friendship, and argues the problems are structural. It starts with real wants, without selling you someone to blame.
  • WatchWon't You Be My Neighbor? Morgan Neville, 2018 ThrowbackThe documentary about Fred Rogers. A friend in the film says his gentleness showed another way of being a man. Remember the sweater and the sneakers?Heads up: Rated PG-13 for some thematic elements and language.
  • WatchThe Karate Kid John G. Avildsen, 1984The Cobra Kai sensei teaches aggression and tells his students to hurt Daniel. Mr. Miyagi teaches self-control. Two ideas of strong, side by side. Rated PG.Heads up: Daniel gets beaten up by the Cobra Kai guys.
  • ListenBoys Don't Cry The Cure, 1979A guy pretends he's fine after losing someone. Robert Smith said English boys were taught not to show emotion, and he pushed back on that.
  • ListenI'll Make a Man Out of You Donny Osmond, 1998 ThrowbackThe Mulan training song. Every recruit fails to climb the pole and get the arrow. Mulan figures it out. Listen again with "what strong actually looks like" in mind.
  • MakeMake a playlist of men who say what they feel. Five songs, any genre. Name it after the line from the lesson you most want to remember.
