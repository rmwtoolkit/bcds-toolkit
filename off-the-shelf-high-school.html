Myth or fact?

A normal B12 test means nitrous hasn’t hurt your nerves.

AnswerMyth. In 20 patients with spinal cord damage from nitrous, the average B12 test looked normal.

Swart et al., European Journal of Neurology, 2021

How nitrous does damage

From balloon to numb feet.

Nitrous knocks out B12, which nerves need to keep their coating. Tingling toes are an early warning sign.

Myth or fact?

You can die from sniffing a duster the first time.

AnswerFact. It’s called sudden sniffing death. The chemicals make the heart oversensitive to adrenaline.

Bass, JAMA, 1970

Take apart the package

Read the label like a skeptic.

“Natural” and “legal” are sales words. “Lab tested” doesn’t say for what. “Not for human consumption” usually means “we don’t want to be responsible.”

Call 911 right away if someone passes out and won't wake up, isn't breathing normally, has blue lips, a seizure, chest pain, or an irregular heartbeat, or hits their head when they fall. Give naloxone if kratom, 7-OH, or tianeptine was involved. Poison Control is 1-800-222-1222.

Dusters

A duster can stop a heart on the first try. Keep the person sitting, and don’t chase or startle them.

When to call 911

Fainting, a seizure, chest pain, trouble breathing, or someone who won’t wake up.
