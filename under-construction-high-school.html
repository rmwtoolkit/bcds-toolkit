Agree or disagree?

“Most adults expect to change a lot in the next ten years.”

AnswerThey don’t. At every age, people said they’d changed a lot in ten years and expected to change very little next. They were wrong. The you at 25 will probably surprise you.

Quoidbach, Gilbert & Wilson, Science, 2013

Three moments

What would you actually say?

Ellie

Her family has always said she’ll be pre-med like her older sister. She’s starting to love design.

“Can I show you some design work I’m into? I’d also like to hear what you hope for me.”

Gabe

A classmate asks where he’s from. “Ohio.” “No, where are you really from?”

“Ohio. My family’s from the Philippines, if that’s what you mean.” Or just “Ohio.” He doesn’t owe anyone his story.

Sam

A friend tells Sam, nervously, that they’re questioning their gender.

“Thanks for telling me. I’m here. Anything you want me to do, or not do?”

Knowing where you come from

Across 46 studies, minority youth who felt good about their group had fewer symptoms of depression and better self-esteem. Everyone has a cultural story, and mixed, adopted or immigrant-family students may be building a few at once.
