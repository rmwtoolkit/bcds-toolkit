Faculty Toolkit

How this toolkit was made

Written by Dr. Rick Wilson, with AI-assisted drafting. Every lesson is reviewed and edited by Dr. Wilson.

What Dr. Wilson does

  • Chooses every topic and the order they're taught in
  • Writes the stories, scenarios and talking points from his work with students
  • Makes the clinical calls: what's safe to teach in a group, what belongs with a counselor
  • Reviews and edits every slide and student page before it goes out

What AI helps with

  • First drafts of wording, which then get rewritten
  • Finding research to check
  • Building slide and page layouts
  • Drawing the cartoons

No student's name, record or story is ever typed into an AI tool.

About the research

Every fact in a lesson lists its source, so you can look it up yourself. If you find a number that's wrong or out of date, please tell Dr. Wilson and it gets fixed in every lesson that uses it.

Why say any of this?

We ask students to be honest about how they use AI in their work. The adults who made this toolkit should do the same.
