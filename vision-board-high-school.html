Vision Board

What do you want this year to hold? You're the hub. School is one spoke. Build a wheel that fits your life, then fill it in.

This stays on your laptop. Nobody sees it unless you show them.

First, build your wheel

Pick the spokes that fit your life

Turn spokes on or off, or name your own. Six to eight is plenty.

Your wheel changes as you pick spokes.

Then, fill it in

One thing you want from each spoke

Or tap any spoke on the wheel.

One to start

Pick one spoke to start this month

What could get in the way?

If then

Your word for the year

Pick up to three words, then star one

The starred word goes big on your board. Don't see yours? Add it.

Last, make it a board

Turn your wheel into a vision board

Every spoke you filled is a tile. Tap a tile to pick its color, add a graphic or a picture, or make it bigger. Add anything else that belongs on your year.

The picture works as a lock screen or wallpaper. The PDF prints on one page. Pictures come from your laptop and stay on it.

Erased. Your wheel and board are blank again.

Some spokes feel empty or heavy right now? That’s real, and it’s a good reason to talk with Dr. Wilson or Ms. Garcia, or any adult you trust.

When school is closed, call or text 988. In an emergency, call 911. This page isn’t monitored. Nobody at school sees what you tap or type here.
