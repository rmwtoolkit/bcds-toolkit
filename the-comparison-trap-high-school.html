Why it still lands

A feed shows a thin, edited slice of other people’s lives, and you set it against everything you know about your own. People also underestimate how often their friends feel sad, lonely or behind, because those moments mostly stay hidden. The feed makes that blind spot bigger.

What the research says
  • Likes are contagious. Teens liked the same photo more when it already showed lots of likes.
  • Changing what you see works. College students who capped each app at ten minutes a day for three weeks felt less lonely and less down.
Skip these
  • “Everyone else is fine.”You’re comparing to a version of them that doesn’t exist.
  • “Likes show how much people like me.”They track timing, the algorithm and herd behavior.
  • “I just need to stop caring.”Trying not to care rarely works. Changing what you see does.
