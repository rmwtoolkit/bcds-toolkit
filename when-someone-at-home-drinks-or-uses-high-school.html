How many U.S. kids live with a parent who has a substance use disorder?

AnswerAbout 1 in 4. Kids in this spot are in every school, on every team. You are not the only one.

McCabe et al., JAMA Pediatrics, 2025

How much of the risk comes from genes?

AnswerAbout half. A real reason to be careful with alcohol and drugs. Not destiny: the other half includes what you do.

Verhulst, Neale & Kendler, Psychological Medicine, 2015

What would help Jordan?

Jordan, a junior

Skips lunch to finish homework, works most nights, hasn’t had anyone over since freshman year. Jordan’s mom drinks most nights, and Jordan covers for her with the school.

Jordan might feel exhausted, embarrassed, protective. One adult at school who knows can take some of the weight.

The promise

Jordan tells a friend, then asks: “Promise you won’t tell anyone?”

“I won’t spread it around. If you’re ever not safe, I’ll get an adult. That’s me being your friend.”

You can also talk to the teacher or advisor who shared this, or any adult you trust.

Talk to someone if

  • you're covering for a parent, or doing adult jobs that cost you sleep or grades
  • a parent in recovery seems off and you're carrying it alone
  • you've noticed you drink or use to get through weekends

Get help right away if someone at home hurts you, threatens you, or you feel unsafe, or if you don't have food, heat, or a sober adult to take care of you. If a parent can't be woken, has trouble breathing, or may have overdosed, call 911, roll them on their side, and give naloxone (Narcan) if opioids could be involved. Massachusetts' Good Samaritan law protects the caller and the person overdosing from drug possession charges. The Childhelp hotline is 1-800-422-4453, any time.

Alateen, part of Al-Anon, runs free meetings in person and online for teens affected by someone else's drinking: al-anon.org.

NACoA has resources for kids of parents with any addiction: nacoa.org.

Other ways in

The research says steady adults and a wider circle make the difference. These stories show what that looks like.

  • ReadMatilda Roald Dahl, 1988 ThrowbackMatilda's parents ignore her. Her teacher, Miss Honey, doesn't. The Kauai study found kids did better when they had support outside the house. Miss Honey is that support.Heads up: Matilda's parents are neglectful and cruel to her, played mostly for laughs.
  • WatchInstant Family Sean Anders, 2018Fifteen-year-old Lizzie is used to looking after her two younger siblings, because their mom couldn't. When their mom relapses, Lizzie has to let other adults take care of her. Based on the director's own family.Heads up: Rated PG-13 for thematic elements, sexual material, language and some drug references. A parent's drug addiction and relapse.
  • ListenSurface Pressure Lin-Manuel Miranda, sung by Jessica Darrow, 2021Luisa is the strong one, the one everyone leans on, until it's too much. It's the caretaker role from the lesson, set to a beat.
  • ListenBecause of You Kelly Clarkson, 2005Clarkson wrote it at 16 about her parents' divorce. In the video she catches herself repeating their fights and stops. Half the story is in your hands.
  • LookHey, Kiddo Jarrett J. Krosoczka, 2018A graphic memoir. Krosoczka's mom was addicted, so his grandparents raised him, and drawing got him through. He grew up to make comics. A National Book Award finalist.Heads up: A parent's addiction, with some drinking and family conflict.
  • MakeMap your own Kauai circle. Draw yourself in the center, then add rings for extended family, neighbors, school, and anyone else who shows up for you. Who could you add next?
