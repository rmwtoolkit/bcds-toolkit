What would help most?

Ava

Deferred from her early decision school the same night her close friend got in. Her friend wants to celebrate this weekend.

Congratulate her friend. Say she needs a day. Celebrate when she’s ready. Rereading the portal every night won’t change it.

Eli

Wants to go to school three states away. His parents want him to commute to a state university to save money. Every talk turns into a fight.

Run the net price calculators together and bring in his counselor. The money talk is part of fit, not a betrayal. Staying close is a real choice too.

Mia

Four APs, two sports and a job. Sleeping five hours a night. Dropped the art class she loved because it “doesn’t count.”

Drop one commitment, protect sleep, bring back the art. A few things you care about say more than a long list.

More, if you want it

Your feed shows a narrow slice

Of 1,364 four-year colleges, only 17 admitted fewer than 1 in 10 applicants, and more than half admitted at least two-thirds. Decision-day posts come mostly from those 17.

The name matters less than people think. Mostly.

Students who got into similar schools but chose differently ended up earning about the same on average. The boost was real for some groups, including first-gen students. A professor who cared about you mattered more than school type.

Fit has four parts
  • Academic fit
  • Financial fit
  • Life fit
  • Odds

Prestige isn’t one of them.

Other ways in

Plenty of people have written about getting into college, and about what matters once you're there. One of these you probably sang along to.

  • ReadWhere You Go Is Not Who You'll Be Frank Bruni, 2015A New York Times columnist uses data and life stories to argue that lots of kinds of colleges are good launching points. It's the Dale and Krueger finding, at book length.
  • WatchHigh School Musical 3: Senior Year Disney, 2008 ThrowbackTroy is pulled between basketball and theater, and between what his dad wants and what he wants. He finds a school that lets him do both. Rated G.
  • WatchMonsters University Pixar, 2013 ThrowbackMike and Sulley get expelled and still end up on the scare floor, by way of the mailroom. What you do once you get somewhere matters more. Rated G.
  • ListenWide Open Spaces The Chicks, 1998A girl leaves home while her parents worry at the door. She needs room to make big mistakes. Susan Gibson wrote it after leaving home for college in Montana.
  • MakeMake two lists on one page. Left side: the schools people expect you to talk about. Right side: what you want a place to have, like a professor who knows your name or a cost your family can handle. Circle which list is yours.
