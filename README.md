# BCDS Student Toolkit

Student-facing lessons and check-ins for Beaver Country Day School, grades 6 to 12. Most topics have a middle school (6 to 8) and an upper school (9 to 12) version. The Launch Ready series for seniors, Study Drugs, and Mushrooms and Psychedelics are upper school only.

- Open `index.html` to browse all topics. Every lesson links back to it with "All topics."
- Each lesson is one self-contained HTML file. Nothing a student types is saved or sent anywhere.
- Every lesson includes 988, the Crisis Text Line, the ANAD eating disorders helpline, and an educational-use disclaimer.

For educational use. Not therapy, counseling, or a diagnosis.
