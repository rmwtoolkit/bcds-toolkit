Why “they didn’t say no” proves nothing

Freezing is a reflex, not a choice. In a Swedish study of people seen after a sexual assault, 70% said their body locked up. Signals get misread too: in a speed-dating study, people regularly misjudged each other’s interest. Asking fixes it. Drinking never moves the blame onto the person who drank.

Three ways to step in
  • Distract: “Come to the bathroom with me.”
  • Delegate: get her friends, the host, an adult.
  • Direct: say it plainly.

Schools that trained students to step in had less sexual violence afterward.

If a friend tells you

Listen. Believe them. “It’s not your fault.” Let them name it. Offer to walk with them to a counselor.

Skip these
  • “Couples don’t need to ask”Asking gets easier when you know each other. It doesn’t become optional.
  • “Guys always want it”Anyone can be pressured. A guy’s no counts the same.
  • “Asking kills the mood”Pressure kills the mood. A quick question says you care.
Other ways in

Asking turns up in a lot of good stories. These show what it looks like, and what it looks like when someone doesn't.

  • WatchTea Consent Emmeline May and Blue Seat Studios, 2015A two-minute cartoon that swaps sex for a cup of tea. If they're unconscious, don't make them tea. It walks through capacity and ongoing yes better than most lectures.
  • WatchBrave Pixar, 2012 ThrowbackMerida's parents line up three suitors for her. She refuses to be handed over to any of them. A yes has to be freely given, even in a castle. Rated PG.
  • ListenNO Meghan Trainor, 2016A guy at a club won't take the hint, and she gives him the clearest answer there is. Trainor called it an anthem about telling a dude she's good on her own.
  • ListenRespect Aretha Franklin, 1967Older than your parents and still the plainest version of the idea. Find out what it means to the other person.
