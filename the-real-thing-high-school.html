Am I ready? (just for you)
  • Is this something I want, or something I feel I’m supposed to want?
  • Am I feeling pressure from a partner, friends, or myself?
  • Could I talk openly with this person about boundaries?
  • Would I feel okay saying no, or stopping partway?
  • Does this fit my own values? Yours, and your family’s or faith’s if those matter to you.
  • Am I getting my ideas from real life, or from a screen?

For most people your age, the honest answer is “not yet.” That’s a strong answer.

What the screen leaves out

Asking and checking in. Nerves. Laughing when it’s awkward. Ordinary bodies. Stopping when someone isn’t into it. Rough or painful stuff isn’t normal just because it’s on a screen. Saying yes to sex is never saying yes to being hurt.

Protection basics

Many STIs have no symptoms, so testing is the only way to know. Condoms used every time lower the risk of STIs and pregnancy. The pill prevents pregnancy, not STIs. Not having sex is the only 100% protection.

Skip these
  • “Rough means passionate”Aggression isn’t desire. Real partners check in.
  • “No just means convince me”No means no. Pressure isn’t persuasion.
  • “Bodies are supposed to look like that”Performers are cast, lit and edited. Real bodies vary.
