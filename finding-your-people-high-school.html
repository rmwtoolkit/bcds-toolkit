More, if you want it

The hours

One researcher estimates about 50 hours together to become casual friends, about 90 to become friends, and over 200 to become close. The walk to the parking lot and food after the meet count.

The liking gap

After a first conversation, people underestimate how much the other person liked them. Most people also want to go deeper than small talk, and assume the other person doesn’t.

One close friend beats a big following

Teens with a strong close friendship at 15 had more self-worth and less anxiety and depression in their mid-20s. Popularity didn’t give that benefit. Groups look sealed from outside. Up close, they’re single friendships.

Other ways in

Plenty of stories are about the slow part, when the hours haven't added up yet. A couple of these go back to when you were small.

  • ReadAnne of Green Gables L.M. Montgomery, 1908Anne shows up in a new town wanting one "kindred spirit." She finds Diana, and it takes a lot of afternoons together to become what she hoped for.Heads up: Matthew, who adopted Anne, dies near the end of the book.
  • WatchNapoleon Dynamite Jared Hess, 2004Napoleon has nobody. Then Pedro transfers in, and Deb keeps coming by. Nobody here is popular, and the friendships still hold. Rated PG.
  • WatchLilo & Stitch Disney, 2002 ThrowbackLilo doesn't fit in with the girls at hula class, so she adopts a dog who turns out to be an alien. Ohana means family, and nobody gets left behind. Rated PG.
  • ListenRainbow Connection Kermit the Frog (Paul Williams and Kenneth Ascher), 1979 ThrowbackA frog alone on a log, singing that someday he'll find the others who are looking for the same thing he is.
  • ListenLean on Me Bill Withers, 1972The kind of friend from the 200-hour end of the scale. The one you'd call at 1am.
  • LookPumpkinheads Rainbow Rowell and Faith Erin Hicks, 2019Deja and Josiah have worked the same pumpkin patch every fall for four years. Lots of hours, and on their last night they find out what those hours added up to. A graphic novel.
  • LookAmerican Born Chinese Gene Luen Yang, 2006Jin is one of the only Asian kids at his school. When a new kid from Taiwan sits near him, Jin isn't sure he wants the friendship. Look at how close he comes to missing it. A graphic novel that won the Printz Award.Heads up: Includes racist teasing and a character drawn as a harsh stereotype, on purpose, so the book can take it apart.
  • MakeDraw the hours scale from the lesson, 0 to 250. Put the people in your life on it in pencil. Pick one person near 50 and write down where you could log ten more hours with them this month.
