More, if you want it

Repair looks different in every family

Some families say “I was wrong.” Some fix it by showing up, cooking, or acting normal again. All of these are real repair.

Why it’s so hard

People who refused to apologize felt more powerful afterward. The friendship got nothing.

Sorry without change

An apology that keeps repeating with no change isn’t repair. Say it once, simply: “It doesn’t count if it keeps happening.” If it continues, talk to an adult.

Other ways in

Some of the best apologies in fiction are mostly action. These get it.

  • ReadPride and Prejudice Jane Austen, 1813Darcy's letter explains himself but doesn't fix much. What changes things is what he does next, quietly paying to clean up the Lydia mess. Offer to repair, then do it.
  • WatchAvatar: The Last Airbender Nickelodeon, 2005 ThrowbackIn "The Southern Raiders," Zuko tries to earn back Katara's trust by helping her find the man who killed her mother. She doesn't forgive him because he says sorry. She forgives him after he shows up. Rated TV-Y7.Heads up: The episode centers on Katara's grief and anger over her mother's death, years before the story.
  • WatchLittle Women Greta Gerwig, 2019Amy burns Jo's manuscript, a real hurt between sisters who share a house and can't avoid each other. Rated PG for thematic elements and brief smoking.
  • ListenBack to December Taylor Swift, 2010Swift called it her first apology song. It names exactly what she did that night, with no "if" and no "but." Acknowledging responsibility, which the Lewicki study ranked first.
  • LookAmerican Born Chinese Gene Luen Yang, 2006Jin hurts his best friend Wei-Chen badly and lets the friendship go. The way back takes Jin owning who he is first.Heads up: Uses a deliberately offensive Chinese caricature to make a point about racism, and includes a fight between friends.
  • MakeWrite the fight you're stuck on in the third person, using both names, the way the lesson suggests. Then write the apology, putting the six parts in the order the research ranked them. Leave off asking for forgiveness.
