Three rooms

How do you talk with friends, with a teacher, and at home?

Reading a room and adjusting is a skill.

Guess first

People who hold two cultures together. Do they do better, worse, or the same?

AnswerBetter, on average. Across 83 studies, blending two cultures went with better well-being. Switching all day still takes energy, and being called “fake” adds stress.

Nguyen & Benet-Martínez, 2013; McCluney et al., 2021; Durkee & Williams, 2015

Two moments

What would you say?

Jesse

Jesse switches into his class voice to ask a teacher a question. A friend laughs: “Why you talk so proper? You’re fake.”

“Both are me. I just read the room.” Or the friend can let it go: switching isn’t fake.

Ava

Her friends are planning a ski weekend. She says she’s busy, because saying money’s tight feels harder.

A friend who notices can plan a free one too: “Movie night at mine Saturday?”

Other ways in

Writers, filmmakers, musicians and artists have been showing what it's like to live between rooms for a long time.

  • ReadAmerican Born Chinese Gene Luen Yang, 2006A graphic novel where Jin tries hard to be the kind of kid his new school wants, and a cousin shows up who is every stereotype he's running from. Funny and sharp about wanting to fit in.
  • ReadDarius the Great Is Not Okay Adib Khorram, 2018Darius is half Iranian, feels not American enough at home and not Persian enough in Iran, and finally meets a friend who doesn't need him to switch.
  • WatchSelena Gregory Nava, 1997Her father explains the bind of being Mexican American: too Mexican for some, not Mexican enough for others, judged on both your English and your Spanish. Rated PG.
  • WatchThe Hate U Give George Tillman Jr., 2018Starr is one person in her neighborhood and another at her mostly white, wealthy private school, until something happens that makes her stop splitting herself.Heads up: Rated PG-13 for mature themes, violence including a police shooting, drug content and language.
  • ListenI Am Not My Hair India.Arie, 2006Not your hair, your skin, or what other people expect: she sings about being the person underneath all of it. Nominated for a Grammy for Best R&B Song.
  • ListenCode Switch NPR podcast, since 2016Journalists talking honestly about race, culture and identity, named after exactly what this lesson is about. Pick an episode title that grabs you.
  • LookPortraits by Kehinde Wiley Kehinde Wiley, 2000s to nowHe paints young Black people from the street in the poses of old European royalty and saints. Two worlds on one canvas, and neither one wins. He also painted President Obama's official portrait.
  • MakeDraw your rooms. A box for each place you spend time, sized by how much you switch there. Color the ones that feel like home games. Keep it or toss it.
